{{ l.label }}
Layers, not shadows

The logo's opacity steps — 1, 0.65, 0.35 — are the Echo lens: the loop is solid, the lines around it fall back one layer at a time. Depth comes from translucency, so surfaces stay flat and cheap to draw.

The originalEnjoyYou
Two ends of one gradient

Blue, where the logo starts, is the original voice. Violet, where it ends, is you. The whole gradient is Enjoy itself — the logo, Play, primary buttons, the goal ring and Pro. Everything else is ink.

The soft triangle

The rounded play triangle is the play glyph everywhere, the cover art for media without artwork (each item crops the mark differently), and the illustration in empty states.

Colour

Never by hue alone

Blue and violet sit close on the colour wheel, so they are also kept apart by weight, like the logo: the original is the light, translucent layer; you are the solid layer in front. Pitch lines, buttons and the loop all follow that rule, and both pairings keep a lightness gap in light and dark.
