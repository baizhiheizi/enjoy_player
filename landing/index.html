Three ways through every line.

Listen until you hear every word, say it back until it sounds right, then hide the text and test your ears. Switch any time — the line stays where you are.

Interactive transcripts

Your media, with every line in time.

Open an MP4 or an audio file, or paste a YouTube link. Import .srt / .vtt, use YouTube captions, or let AI transcribe and align it line by line.

  • Local files play locally — nothing is uploaded to watch
  • No subtitles? AI transcribes and aligns them
  • Translations under each line, cached on your device
  • IPA over every word when you want it

Pronunciation assessment

Feedback down to the sound you got wrong.

Every take is scored the moment you stop recording — overall, then accuracy, fluency, completeness and prosody, then every word. Tap a word to see which sound slipped.

  • Four dimensions, every word colour-coded
  • Your pitch laid over the original's
  • Keep every take and watch the score climb

Lookup & vocabulary

Look it up in context. Keep it for good.

Select any word or phrase for a translation, a dictionary entry and an AI note on what it means in this scene. One tap saves it — with the line it came from — to a deck that comes back when you're about to forget.

  • Translation, dictionary and contextual explanation
  • Spaced repetition with three honest buttons
  • Lookups in 15 languages; Anki export on paid plans

Craft

Practise what you actually want to say.

Say or type something from your day in your own language. Craft rewrites it the way a native speaker would, reads it aloud, and drops it into the player so you can shadow it like any other line.

  • Idiomatic rewrites, not word-for-word translation
  • Natural voices to listen to and copy
  • Practise it in Echo, scored like everything else
